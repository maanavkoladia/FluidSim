\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

We plan to evaluate the idea by defining clear and measurable criteria that align with our project goals, such as 
accuracy, efficiency, usability, and scalability. The prototype will be tested against baseline methods or existing solutions to determine whether it 
offers meaningful improvements. We will also perform stress testing under different scenarios to assess robustness and reliability. Where applicable, we 
will gather feedback from peers, mentors, or potential users to gain insights into practicality and ease of use. By combining 
quantitative performance metrics with qualitative feedback, we can comprehensively evaluate both the technical effectiveness and the real-world 
applicability of our idea.

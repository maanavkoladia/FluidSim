\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

A grid-based fluid simulation is important because they 
have broad applications across the industry and entertainment. For 
example, real-time graphics and games have believable 
water that doesn’t appear to compress, expand
, or slowly vanish which is essential for quality of 
the game. Another example is in the film industry
, where this can save artists time by reducing artifacts 
that would otherwise need manual correction. Engineering fields also 
rely on fast, grid-based incompressible fluid solvers 
for tasks such as HVAC design, flood prediction, 
maritime engineering, and digital twins, where interactive or low-cost pre-visualization is 
valuable before high-fidelity simulations. Robotics, medical training, and AR/VR systems benefit as well,
since real-time mass-conserving simulations help with accurate interaction feedback in dynamic environments. 
Ultimately, advancing grid-based fluid simulations ensures more realistic, efficient, and reliable tools that 
enhance creativity, improve decision-making, and expand what’s possible across both entertainment and engineering applications.
